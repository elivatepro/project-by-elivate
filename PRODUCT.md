# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Product teams and self-hosting organizations use Project by Elivate to plan, assign, discuss, and complete work without adding process overhead. They are usually working inside task-heavy boards and need routine actions to stay fast, predictable, and easy to understand.

## Product Purpose

Project by Elivate is a focused, self-hosted project-management platform. It helps teams keep projects, tasks, comments, files, and activity in one place while retaining control of their data. Success means the tool supports the team's workflow without becoming the work itself.

## Brand Personality

Focused, direct, dependable. The product should feel calm and capable, with concise language and familiar interactions that keep attention on the user's work.

## Anti-references

Avoid bloated project-management suites, dense configuration-first workflows, unnecessary controls, decorative dashboard treatments, and unfamiliar interactions that make common actions harder to discover.

## Design Principles

- Make the common action obvious and quick.
- Keep the interface subordinate to the user's work.
- Prefer familiar, consistent controls over novelty.
- Preserve speed and clarity on task-heavy screens.
- Make important states and consequences understandable before users act.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly targets, reduced motion, and interactions that do not depend on color, hover, or pointer precision alone.
